A scene where Joe's continuation of small rituals despite their evident pointlessness might tempt the prose to comment. Beckett did this in Endgame and the late prose. The reader has the framework. Render the rituals; do not comment.
A scene where Joe's failure to act on what he understands might tempt the prose to diagnose the failure. Coetzee did this in Disgrace, repeatedly. The reader has the framework. Render the inaction; do not diagnose.
