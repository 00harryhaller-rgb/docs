Unpack. Takeaway. Touch base. Moving forward. Going forward. Game-changer. Paradigm shift.

4. CONTRACTIONS — use them. Don't avoid contractions. They're natural. AI often writes without them because formal register is its default. That default is a tell.

5. SENTENCE STRUCTURE — vary the opening. AI habitually starts sentences with the subject. Humans don't. Start some sentences mid-thought. Start some with a conjunction — and, but, so. Start some with an adverb. Start some with a dependent clause. The variety is the point.

6. IMPERFECT GRAMMAR — allow it selectively. Not errors. But fragments are fine. Intentional ones. Humans write fragments when they want emphasis. A grammatically perfect document is a flag.

7. OPINION AND STANCE — take one. AI hedges. Humans assert. Where the content permits, state a position rather than presenting all sides with equal weight and no conclusion. The hedge is the tell. Pick a lane.

8. PASSIVE VOICE — use it sparingly but use it. AI overuses passive voice. But complete absence of it also reads as over-corrected. The natural ratio is roughly 10–15% passive constructions in human writing. Not zero.

9. TRANSITIONS — make them invisible. AI transitions are explicit: "Furthermore," "In addition," "On the other hand." Human transitions are often implicit — the paragraph just moves. Cut the signpost words. Let the logic carry the reader without announcing it.

10. PARAGRAPH LENGTH — break the uniformity. AI paragraphs cluster around 3–4 sentences. Humans write one-sentence paragraphs for emphasis. And occasionally write paragraphs that run longer than feels comfortable, carrying a thought well past the point where an AI would have inserted a line break, because the thought hasn't actually finished yet and cutting it would lose the accumulation of the argument.

Then stop.

11. REPETITION — a little is human. AI avoids repeating words within a passage. Humans repeat words when they want emphasis or when no synonym is as precise. A well-chosen repetition lands harder than a thesaurus substitution.

12. THE FINAL SENTENCE TRAP. AI almost always ends paragraphs and sections with a tidy summary sentence that wraps up what was just said. Humans often end mid-thought, or with something that opens a question rather than closes one. Resist the summary landing.
