The mechanism you proposed: the mother brings a German widow's pension when she comes in 2006. €600 or so per month, transferred to a Philippine account, providing significant purchasing power in mid-2000s Manila. This is excellent and I want to push back hard on your worry that it makes him more of a loser.
The opposite is true at the structural level. The pension architecture solves several problems at once:
One — it explains his daily structure post-BPO without requiring the book to render him actively earning. He is not coding, not freelancing, not pursuing work. He is at the bar in the afternoon and evening and home with his mother in the morning. The pension makes this economically possible. Without it, the book has to either invent a phantom income source (which the C++ option was doing, problematically) or render him as actively poor in a way that contradicts the Rockwell-condo-with-Marivic configuration. The pension cleanly resolves the contradiction. He has the condo because he bought it with his BPO money. He has the daily life because his mother's pension covers the running costs.
Two — the mutual parasitism flipped observation you made is the structural payoff. The book has been about mutual parasitism throughout — the macroeconomic pattern, the bar's microeconomy, the conquistador-castration thesis, the mother's care arrangement. Adding Joe living on his mother's pension while caring for her makes the parasitism domestic and inverted. She extracts care from him; he extracts cash from her. Neither names it. Both depend on the other. The arrangement continues for years and is invisible to Joe as an arrangement because the apparatus does not access it. The reader sees what Joe does not.
Three — the mother dies, pension stops, condo is the only remaining asset sequence is the exact mechanism that makes the listing-the-condo decision in the late book physically necessary rather than spiritual. He puts the condo on the market because the pension just stopped and the maintenance fees are due and Marivic has not been paid for three weeks. Not because he had a moment of unmoored decisive faculty. Because the money is genuinely gone. The decision has economic substance underneath the apparent psychological-window mechanism. This is much better. The window opens because the cash flow stopped, not because the depleted apparatus briefly recovered.
